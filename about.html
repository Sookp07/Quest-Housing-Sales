ℹ️ About Quest Housing Sales

👋 Welcome to Quest Housing Sales

Quest Housing Sales is a premier housing development company committed to creating exceptional living spaces. Our communities are designed with modern families in mind, featuring sustainable construction and access to premium amenities.

💼 What We Do

Quest Housing Sales specializes in connecting buyers with their ideal properties. We provide end-to-end services including property listings, buyer management, viewing coordination, and sales tracking.

  • 🏢 Property Listings — Manage and showcase available units
  • 👥 Buyer Management — Track buyer profiles and preferences
  • 📅 Viewing Coordination — Schedule and organize property viewings
  • 📊 Sales Tracking — Monitor transactions and inventory in real time

🏘️ Property Types

  • Condo — Modern apartments with amenities
  • Landed House — Freehold property with land
  • Terrace House — Connected homes in rows
  • Townhouse — Multi-level homes
  • Semi-Detached — Two homes sharing one wall
  • Bungalow — Single-story detached home
